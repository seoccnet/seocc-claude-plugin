# Privacy

The SEOcc plugin for Claude collects no data.

- **No collection or telemetry.** The plugin is made of text instructions only. It has no code that runs on your computer, holds no credentials, and never contacts the plugin's authors or any vendor server.
- **One connection, added by you.** Claude talks to SEOcc only through the MCP server you add yourself: your own self-hosted installation. Its data stays on your hosting.
- **What reaches Claude.** When you ask a question, Claude reads the SEOcc data it needs (search performance, pages, keywords, audit results and similar) within the scopes you approved. That data is then part of your Claude conversation and is handled under your agreement with Anthropic.
- **Sign-in.** You connect with OAuth on your own SEOcc, or with a token you create there. SEOcc stores only a hash of each access token. You can revoke access at any time in SEOcc under **Integrations → AI access**.
- **Writes.** If you grant write scopes, Claude can save change notes, tasks, briefs, finding status and page watches in your SEOcc, only after you approve each one. SEOcc audits every write with the connection's name.
- **Retention.** The plugin keeps nothing. Your SEOcc installation keeps your data under your own retention settings.
- **Not for children.** The plugin is a business tool and isn't directed at anyone under 18.
